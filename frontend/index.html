Chapter II

Book a free demo

Compare qualifications, hourly fees and ratings, then request a demo class in one of the teacher's open time slots.

  • The demo class costs ₹0
  • The teacher accepts or declines your request
  • When they accept, a private chat opens between the two of you
  • Open any profile to see qualifications, slots and reviews

Chapter IV

How we keep it safe

  • Documents are reviewed. ID proof, address proof and a certificate are checked by an admin before a profile can be found.
  • Ratings come from real classes. Only students with a completed booking can rate a teacher.
  • Payments are confirmed by our server. Online payments go through Razorpay; we never see your card details.
  • Problems get a human review. Report a teacher from any accepted or completed booking.

Questions people ask

Request a demo from any teacher's profile and pick a time from their open slots. The demo costs ₹0. If the teacher accepts and you want to continue, you can set up regular classes with them.
Teachers upload an ID proof, an address proof and a teaching certificate or degree. An admin reviews these documents before the profile appears in search results.
Once a teacher accepts a booking that has a fee, you can pay online through Razorpay. You can also pay the teacher in cash, and the teacher records it. The payment status of every booking is shown on your dashboard.
Yes. When a teacher accepts your booking, a private chat opens between the two of you on your dashboards. Use it to agree on timing and study material.
If a teacher cancels a class you have paid for, the payment is refunded. If you cancel yourself, you are refunded when you cancel at least 24 hours before the class. For a no-show, report the teacher from your dashboard and an admin will review it.

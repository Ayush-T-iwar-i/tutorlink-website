What happens before a teacher is listed

A teacher appears in search only after an admin has approved their profile. Here is how safety works on TutorLink today.

  • Documents are reviewed

    Teachers upload an ID proof, an address proof and a teaching certificate or degree. An admin reviews them before the profile can be found in search.

  • Ratings come from real classes

    Only students with a completed booking can rate a teacher. Students can edit or delete their review for 24 hours, and teachers can reply to it.

  • Payments are confirmed by our server

    Online payments go through Razorpay, and a booking is marked paid only after our server has confirmed the payment. TutorLink never sees your card details.

  • Problems get a human review

    You can report a teacher from any accepted or completed booking. An admin looks at the report and can take the teacher out of search.
